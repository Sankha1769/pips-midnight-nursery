How I Got Here

Scene 1 · The Yawn. Once upon a bedtime, a very small yawn echoed through the Midnight Nursery. That was me! I had no name yet, just a nightcap, two sleepy lights for eyes, and a giggle I couldn’t stop.
Scene 2 · The Silence. The nursery was quiet. Too quiet! Little ghosts couldn’t sleep, and I hate silence. So I tapped a spoon on a star, and it went ting. My first lullaby.
Scene 3 · The Job. The Head Dreamer found me humming on the roof. “You’re clumsy,” she said, “but your dreams glow.” She gave me a tiny toolbox. Junior Dream Engineer, Pip. Hehe!
Scene 4 · Tonight. Now I fly across rooftops every night, delivering dreams and fixing nightlights. I still trip on clouds. Come back at bedtime, and I’ll save you a star.
